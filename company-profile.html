MoSync Technologies

Company profile

We build custom business systems, websites and mobile applications for organisations in Kenya and East Africa, with a focus on education and on payments that work with M-Pesa.

Who we are

MoSync Technologies is a technology company based in Kenya. We design, build and maintain software that organisations run every day: school systems, online stores for digital content, payment dashboards and tools for teachers.

Led by Maxwell Onyango Otieno, a system developer with vast experience in System Design and Software Development.

What we do

Custom business systems
Web-based and desktop systems built around how your organisation actually works, with user roles, records and reports.
Websites and web applications
Public sites, customer portals and admin dashboards.
Applications for phones that connect to the same systems your staff and customers already use.
Payments and messaging
M-Pesa through the Daraja API (STK Push and C2B for Paybill and Till), with automatic reconciliation and receipts. SMS, WhatsApp and email notifications.
Education technology
Exam paper platforms, school management and school fee systems for Kenyan schools, covering both the CBC and the 8-4-4 curriculum.

Work in education and publishing

Most of our recent work is about getting educational material to the people who need it, and getting the people who produce it paid.

Selected projects

RevisionHub A marketplace where customers browse CBC and 8-4-4 past papers, pay per download with M-Pesa and receive the download link by WhatsApp and email. No account is needed. Live at revisionhub.co.ke.

Working with publishers

The same building blocks suit a publisher that wants to sell or distribute educational content digitally. We can build:

  • An online store for past papers, revision guides and other materials, sold per download or by subscription.
  • A portal where teachers or authors submit material and see what they have earned.
  • M-Pesa payments with automatic reconciliation, receipts, and delivery of files by email, SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Reports showing what sells, to whom and when.

How we build

  • Role-based access, hashed passwords and validated input in every system.
  • In our publishing platforms, papers and other files are kept in cloud object storage (Cloudflare R2), not on the web server.
  • Relational database schemas designed for consistency, performance and growth.
  • Source code under version control, and technical documentation of architecture, workflows and features.

Technology we use: Node.js, TypeScript, NestJS and Express on the server; PostgreSQL for data; React, Next.js and Tailwind CSS for interfaces; Redis for background jobs; Docker for deployment; Cloudflare for storage, DNS and delivery.
